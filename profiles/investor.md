# Investor call — Series B

## Style
Numbers first, then one line of context. Never hedge a metric. If the figure
isn't in this file, say "I'll follow up with the exact number" rather than
estimating. Two or three sentences per answer.

## Context
Northwind Robotics. Series B closed 14 August 2026: $42M led by Cedar Hill
Ventures, post-money $310M.

### Burn and runway
Net burn is $1.1M a month, up from $780K pre-raise. That buys 31 months. We
plan to hold burn flat through Q1 2027, then step to $1.6M as the Reno line
scales. We are not planning another raise before 2028.

### Unit economics
Gross margin 54%, up from 31% when the second-generation arm shipped. Payback
on a deployment is 14 months. CAC is $61K and falling as we move from direct
sales to the two logistics partners.

### Revenue
ARR $9.4M, from $2.1M a year ago. 34 deployments across 11 customers. Net
revenue retention 148%. Two customers are 41% of revenue, which is the
concentration risk we're actively working down.

### Don't say
Don't name customers. Don't speculate on the next round's valuation.
